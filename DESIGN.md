# POTER Edugames Design Direction

## Identity

POTER Edugames is an official educational game distribution site for children, parents, and educators. It should feel trustworthy and clear first, with a controlled sense of playful technology rather than a generic futuristic landing page.

## Visual language

- Primary mood: friendly educational technology, calm and credible.
- Palette: deep navy and royal blue establish trust; cyan is the single interactive accent; neutral slate supports readability.
- Typography: system sans-serif is retained for fast loading, broad device support, and legibility for parents and administrators.
- Shape language: moderately rounded surfaces with clear hierarchy; reserve pill shapes for compact statuses only.
- Identity motif: one restrained animated phone preview in the public hero, supported by the physical pull-cord theme control.
- Admin surfaces: practical and information-dense, with decoration reduced so logs, filters, and system state remain the focus.

## Dials

ENERGY 2 / RHYTHM 2 / MOTION 2

- ENERGY 2: the public page should feel inviting without becoming visually noisy.
- RHYTHM 2: sections may vary in composition, but navigation and information hierarchy stay predictable.
- MOTION 2: motion is limited to theme feedback, focus, and one calm looping story inside the phone. The story has a pause control and becomes manually stepped when `prefers-reduced-motion` is active.

## Decision reasons

- Blue is used because it communicates reliability for an official download source.
- Cyan is limited to actions and system feedback so it remains meaningful.
- The unframed phone is the single hero visual. Its looping story shows a child learning with nearby parental support, a real game interaction, positive feedback, and a POTER logo transition.
- Parent and child scenes use restrained navy SVG silhouettes with connected limbs so the figures remain clear at phone scale without becoming character-heavy illustration.
- The theme control behaves like a hanging woven cord: layered fibers, a flexible curve, spring return, and a quiet two-note confirmation sound make the physical interaction understandable.
- Light and dark themes are both supported because the site serves mixed audiences and viewing conditions.
- Dashboard decoration stays minimal because administrators need fast scanning and accurate operational data.
